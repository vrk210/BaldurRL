# M4 held-out evaluation (seeds 3000–3999)

Verdict: M4 is a fair fight. MaskablePPO trained 1M steps matches the heuristic (60.5% pooled vs 61.4%, ~0.5 SE apart) and far exceeds random (15.6%). The scaled 2v3 profile landed in the discriminative band.

## Setup

- Stage: `m4` (2v3, M2 rules, terminal reward). Two preset Fighters with per-ally Cleave/Surge/Wind pools vs three enemies sampled at near-Fighter parity (HP 12–24, AC 12–18, attack +3–+7, d4/d6/d8, bonus 2–6); fixed order `ALLY 0, ALLY 1, ENEMY 0–2`.
- Training: MaskablePPO, 1,003,520 steps (1024-step rollouts × 4 envs), terminal reward, seeds 0/1/2, default hyperparameters otherwise. Wall times: 214s, 209s, 211s.
- Evaluation: seeds 3000–3999 (1000 episodes), final models for PPO, heuristic and uniform-random baselines (agent seed 0).
- Artifacts (ignored `runs/`): `runs/m4_ppo_1m_s{0,1,2}/`, `runs/m4_heldout/{heuristic,random,ppo_s0,ppo_s1,ppo_s2}/` (each with `summary.json`, text summary, `episodes.jsonl`, `run_card.json`).

## Results (n=1000 each)

| Agent | Wins | Win rate (SE) | Rounds | Final HP (/40) | SW use | Surge use | Cleave use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heuristic | 614 | 61.40% (1.54%) | 6.16 | 14.44 | 86.7% | 100.0% | 100.0% |
| Random | 156 | 15.60% (1.15%) | 6.18 | 2.98 | 78.5% | 98.6% | 86.6% |
| PPO s0 | 582 | 58.20% (1.56%) | 6.68 | 13.79 | 96.7% | 100.0% | 75.4% |
| PPO s1 | 611 | 61.10% (1.54%) | 6.40 | 14.22 | 96.1% | 100.0% | 97.6% |
| PPO s2 | 622 | 62.20% (1.53%) | 6.43 | 14.49 | 97.8% | 100.0% | 97.1% |

No truncations for any agent. Pooled PPO: 1815/3000 = 60.50% vs heuristic 61.40% (0.9pp gap, SE of difference ≈ 1.8pp). Training-range final evals (seeds 1000–1199, n=200): 54.5/58.5/62.0%, consistent with held-out (no generalization gap beyond noise).

## Target selection

Heuristic first kills spread across slots (338/282/299). PPO seeds vary: s0 (354/371/180) and s1 (338/303/286) spread like the heuristic, while s2 concentrates first kills on slots 0/1 (439/414/80) yet posts the best win rate (62.2%). Targeted-attack totals show the same pattern (s2: 3578/3547/3062). No single targeting scheme dominates at this sample size; all three seeds sit within ~1.5 SE of each other.

## Behavior notes

- Unlike M3, Cleave matters here: s1/s2 use it in ~97% of episodes (heuristic 100%), while s0's lower, later Cleave use (75% at 1.70 living enemies vs the heuristic's 2.95) coincides with its slightly lower win rate (58.2%).
- Second Wind is near-mandatory (87–98% use everywhere vs 36% on M3), confirming fights are close: survivors keep only ~14/40 HP.
- Random's 15.6% (vs 86.3% on M3) confirms the difficulty jump comes from both the third enemy and the scaled profile.

## Repro

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m evaluation.evaluate --stage m4 --agent heuristic --seed-start 3000 --episodes 1000 --format json --output runs/m4_heldout/heuristic/summary.json --trace-dir runs/m4_heldout/heuristic
```
