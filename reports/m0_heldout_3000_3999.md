# M0 held-out evaluation: seeds 3000–3999

The four policies were evaluated on the same 1,000 environment seeds, separate from the PPO training run's checkpoint-selection seeds (1000–1199). PPO predictions were deterministic and used the legal-action mask. This evaluation reused saved PPO models; it did not retrain them.

| Policy | Wins | Win rate | Mean rounds | Mean final Fighter HP | Second Wind use | Action Surge use |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Random (agent seed 0) | 717 | 71.7% | 6.583 | 9.536 | 73.8% | 94.7% |
| Heuristic | 975 | 97.5% | 3.241 | 16.013 | 25.0% | 98.8% |
| PPO, 50k steps | 971 | 97.1% | 3.274 | 15.998 | 40.4% | 98.8% |
| PPO, final 200k steps | 980 | 98.0% | 3.316 | 15.556 | 24.6% | 87.8% |

The final PPO checkpoint won 5 more fights than the heuristic. This small difference in 1,000 episodes is insufficient evidence of a meaningful improvement. Mean HP was lower for final PPO, and it used Action Surge less often.

The heuristic and final PPO both won on 955 seeds. The heuristic alone won on 20; final PPO alone won on 25. None were losses for both. All 45 loss cases had an action as their first trace divergence: in 42, the heuristic used Action Surge while final PPO ended the turn; in 3, the heuristic used Second Wind while final PPO attacked. These are first-choice differences, not proof that the choice caused the eventual outcome. Once the policies diverge, they can consume the seeded dice stream in a different order.

Example: on seed 3009, both initially missed an attack. The heuristic then used Action Surge and won in round 2; final PPO ended the turn, saved Action Surge until round 2, and lost in round 6. On seed 3054, the heuristic's early Action Surge was followed by misses and a loss, while final PPO's delayed use was followed by a win. Inspecting these trajectories helps identify policy patterns without attributing all outcome differences to one action.

Detailed local artifacts are under `runs/heldout/`: each policy has `run_card.json` and `episodes.jsonl`. `heuristic_vs_ppo_final.json` and `ppo_50k_vs_final.json` list loss cases and first divergences. The `runs/` directory is excluded from Git; this report preserves the main findings in the repository.
