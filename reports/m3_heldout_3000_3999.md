# M3 held-out evaluation (seeds 3000–3999)

Verdict: M3 is a ceiling task. MaskablePPO trained 1M steps matches the heuristic (98.8% vs 99.2%, indistinguishable), far above random (86.3%). Notably, PPO wins while barely using Cleave.

## Setup

- Stage: `m3` (2v2, M2 rules, terminal reward). Two preset Fighters with per-ally Cleave/Surge/Wind pools vs two M1B-sampled enemies; fixed order `ALLY 0, ALLY 1, ENEMY 0, ENEMY 1`.
- Training: MaskablePPO, 1,003,520 steps (1024-step rollouts × 4 envs), terminal reward, seeds 0/1/2, default hyperparameters otherwise. Wall times: 185s, 179s, 180s.
- Evaluation: seeds 3000–3999 (1000 episodes), final models for PPO, heuristic and uniform-random baselines (agent seed 0).
- Artifacts (ignored `runs/`): `runs/m3_ppo_1m_s{0,1,2}/`, `runs/m3_heldout/{heuristic,random,ppo_s0,ppo_s1,ppo_s2}/` (each with `summary.json`, text summary, `episodes.jsonl`, `run_card.json`).

## Results (n=1000 each)

| Agent | Wins | Win rate (SE) | Rounds | Final HP (/40) | SW use | Surge use | Cleave use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| PPO s0 | 990 | 99.00% (0.31%) | 3.89 | 32.47 | 20.2% | 90.4% | 21.6% |

No truncations for any agent. Pooled PPO: 2965/3000 = 98.83%, within noise of the heuristic (0.4pp gap, ~1 SE). Training-range final evals (seeds 1000–1199, n=200): 99.5/99.0/99.0%, consistent with held-out.

## Target selection

Targeted-attack counts are near-even for every agent (heuristic 3356/3472; PPO seeds 4098/4201, 4045/4172, 4194/4318), and first-kill slots split roughly evenly (heuristic 534/464; PPO 468/528, 469/526, 511/486). No agent shows a strong slot preference; both enemies die at similar rates.

## Behavior notes

- PPO solves M3 with targeted attacks and largely ignores Cleave (6–33% use vs the heuristic's 100%), yet matches on wins, rounds (~3.9 vs 3.65), and remaining HP (~32 vs 34). On this easy task the extra Cleave damage is unnecessary.
- PPO uses Second Wind less than the heuristic (20–36% vs 36%) and Action Surge slightly less (90–97% vs 100%), consistent with fights ending before resources matter.
- Random's 86.3% confirms the encounter is easy in absolute terms: even uncoordinated play usually wins.
